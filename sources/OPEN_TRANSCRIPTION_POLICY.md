# Open publication of our transcriptions

Completed, independently produced manuscript transcriptions must be published
in this public repository, not left only in private working files. This applies
to the Dead Sea Scrolls and every other source document we transcribe for POB.

## Completion checklist

Before marking a transcription task complete:

1. Commit the machine-readable transcription in the appropriate source directory.
   DSS records must satisfy the existing transcription schema and validation gates.
2. Include the manuscript identifier, archive/item URL, image or page reference,
   transcription method, review status, and revision history. Preserve line/image
   alignment and hashes where the source pipeline supports them.
3. Mark unreadable letters, gaps, proposed restorations, and disputed readings
   explicitly. Do not turn a proposal into a verified reading for publication.
4. Record text rights separately from photograph rights and archive access terms.
   Independently transcribing ancient text does not automatically authorize
   redistribution of the photograph or copying a modern scholarly reconstruction.
   Preserve upstream licenses. If an applicable restriction is unresolved,
   document the specific blocker rather than bypassing it.
5. Apply the repository's CC BY 4.0 license to our copyrightable original
   contributions, such as notes and editorial explanations. Do not assert a new
   copyright over public-domain ancient wording or purely mechanical transcription;
   identify that material as public domain where established. No license here
   overrides third-party rights.
6. Push the completed transcription and provenance to public main and verify
   the public files are accessible. Include their public links in the task result.

Publishing a transcription is separate from adopting its readings into the Bible
translation. Existing textual review and canonical-publication gates still apply.
Generative reconstructions are never transcription evidence.

This policy governs subsequent transcription work; it does not claim that any
queued manuscript has already been transcribed or that every existing draft has
passed review.

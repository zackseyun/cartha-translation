# Ruth: whole-set published DSS comparison

## Scope and result

All four Ruth records in the pinned QDR biblical dataset were compared with
canonical WLC source wording using full published fragment contexts. The
[receipt](../sources/textual_restoration/comparisons/ruth_dss_screen.v1.json)
pins inputs, all tagged references, line accounting and reviewer allocation.
This is a completed **bounded comparison screen**, not a complete diplomatic
collation, all-source census, or adjudication of every variant. No reconstructed
letters become visible evidence. Earlier Ruth 3:15 retention is not reopened.

also remains unresolved. Raw labels and published locators are not merged into
new physical-object identities.

## Translation consequence and next work

The screen found a concrete POB mismatch at 2:21: the current English restricted
the workers to young women while its source and lexical rationale said young
men. 2Q16's published `עם הנערים` preserves the masculine noun. Unlike the
3:15 verb in a gap, this supplies an actual comparison control. Its application
and checks are recorded below.

Prioritize the new lexical/addition/interrogative candidates over spelling-only
work. Before changing Hebrew, compare the appropriate Greek/versional apparatus,
context and transmission alternatives; this screen does not confer source
priority. Ruth 3:16 is especially useful because existing English already offers
an interpretive alternative: the newly compared Hebrew must be distinguished
from an interpretation of unchanged מי. No new image transcription is needed
unless a specific disputed preserved mark becomes decisive.

## Applied English correction: Ruth 2:21

[SIL/UBS's discussion](https://tips.translation.bible/tip_verse/ruth-221/)
supports a generic interpretation, “workers,” and describes feminine versional
readings as harmonization. Its recommendation is an argument, not another
manuscript. The local Rahlfs control uses παιδαρίων at 2:21, contrasting with
κορασίων at 2:8,22 and κορασίοις at 2:23. These Greek grammatical forms are
not themselves a Hebrew gender reconstruction. Root checked these surfaces;
the earlier Ruth report pins the Greek file.

Under the current source-distinction policy, compare three full-verse options:
current “young women,” “young men,” and “workers.” Adopt **young men** to
preserve the source's contrast; keep the generic possibility in the note.
This does not prove the group excluded women, imply Ruth misunderstood Boaz,
or rank an English rendering by doctrinal usefulness. The legitimate
“with”/“by” alternative is retained. The note marker now follows the noun
phrase it explains instead of Ruth's speech introduction.

The independent agent read the policy, full local context and SIL/UBS discussion,
then approved this full-verse correction and recommended retaining the
prepositional alternative. It relied on root for the Cave 2 and Greek controls.
The receipt stores the three full-verse source-distinction checks, with concise
rationales, the exact applied hash and verification results. No further judge
was run to manufacture agreement. Old revision entries and review objects

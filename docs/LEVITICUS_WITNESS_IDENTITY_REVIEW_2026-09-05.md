# Leviticus witness identities: catalogue reassessment

Date: 2026-09-05. Documentation-only follow-up to the
[En-Gedi comparison](EN_GEDI_APPARATUS_COMPARISON_2026-09-05.md).
No new transcription, source selection, English revision or manuscript count
is established here.

## Question and result

Does our legacy `4Q24` label adequately identify the evidence used at
Leviticus 2:8-9? Not without a reassessment warning. The local QDR record remains
a reproducible digital input, but its label must not settle physical identity,
dating or independent support.

Eibert Tigchelaar's *Reconsidering 4Q24 (4QLeviticusᵇ): Two Manuscripts and a
New Fragment*, DOI `10.1163/15685330-12341447`, argues for different scribal
hands: 4Q24a contains Leviticus 1-3, while 4Q24b contains sections of 21-25;
two additional fragments are assigned to 25:44-46. The
[Pretoria repository metadata and abstract](https://repository.up.ac.za/items/e9572d7d-828e-4ed1-adac-18b3b8bc5a0f)
date the work to November 2020. The author's
[KU Leuven publication listing](https://theo.kuleuven.be/en/research/researchers/00062160/view?fromnr=51&pubsonpage=50&pubtype=&sortby=scdate)
identifies the final journal issue as *Vetus Testamentum* 71/2 (2021), 263-273.
These are distinct publication dates, not a discovery in 2026.

Himbaza's 2020 introduction, printed pp. 2-3, lists 4Q24a at 75-50 BCE and
4Q24b at 125-100 BCE. The former's coverage includes Leviticus 2:8-9. That
supports a passage-level association with the proposed 4Q24a, not a verified
crosswalk for every local fragment or a new reading of its ink.

## Coverage implications and decisions

The introduction's 27-manuscript statement is conditional on the split. Its
table includes disputed identifications, alternative dates, quoted/reworked
material and unpublished entries. It is a dated discovery aid, not our current
authenticated or collated census.

1. Preserve `4Q24` as the upstream identifier. Add a sourced, versioned identity
   crosswalk before migrating object IDs. Keep published alternatives and
   unresolved fragments explicit. Never count the legacy whole plus its
   proposed parts as three independent witnesses.
2. For the existing Lev 2:8-9 checks, retain the precise legacy locator
   `4Q24 / f1_7 / lines 29 and 31` and flag the proposed 4Q24a association.
   Do not assign the later-chapter hand's date to this passage. Neither a
   catalogue range nor this reassessment verifies the decisive letters.
3. Use the table to queue identity, genre, date and access checks against
   current institutional records and later literature. Preserve exclusions
   and authenticity disputes; do not infer authenticity from inclusion in a
   2020 publication. Do not convert ambiguous alternatives into union coverage.
4. Apply the same rule across OT and NT: catalogue aliases, scribal hands,
   fragment joins/splits and edition versions require separate fields. A new
   website, transcription or scholarly label is not another ancient vote.

The strongest contrary consideration is that the existing passage-specific
transcription may remain entirely correct despite an outdated object label.
Therefore this pass adds a provenance warning, not a rejection of QDR or a
reason to change POB. Conversely, unchanged words do not excuse an incorrect
identity or date in later adjudication.

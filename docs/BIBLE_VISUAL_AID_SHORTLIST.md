# Bible visual aid shortlist — the must-do catalog

Curated editorial anchors for the visual-aid catalog, governed by the art
direction in [`BIBLE_VISUAL_AIDS.md`](BIBLE_VISUAL_AIDS.md) (show the stage,
not the play; every entry must pass the three gate tests). This is the master
production queue for Codex Image Gen; the heuristic candidates tool is only a
discovery net.

**The absolute set** below is the canon-wide list of verses that should
definitely carry a visual aid: every entry either unlocks a physical reality
the text assumes and modern readers cannot picture, or is a system asset
(tabernacle, temple, maps) that dozens of passages lean on. One asset per row;
reuse anchors are listed inline — publish the same image at every reuse
anchor. Format is a proposal and labels are candidates; the editor generating
each asset owns the final label set (3–5, up to 6 on maps/object plates).

Production order: the original launch set (marked ★) first, then the rest of
the absolute set roughly in canon order, weighting high-traffic books (Gospels,
Acts, Genesis, Exodus, Psalms) ahead of the rest.

Scope: the absolute set covers the 66-book canon. POB's deuterocanonical and
extra-canonical texts are out of scope until the canon is served. The list was
cross-checked against a full heuristic sweep of the POB corpus
(`tools/bible_visual_aid_candidates.py`, ~9,600 canonical keyword candidates);
the sweep is a discovery net and this curated list governs.

## Do not aid

Standing exclusions (per the art direction). Never anchor an aid where the
image would have to show:

- Any miracle, healing, or theophany in progress — walking on water, calming
  the storm, feeding the multitudes, raising the dead, the burning bush as
  event, Pentecost flames.
- The crucifixion, the resurrection morning, the transfiguration, Gethsemane,
  the Last Supper as a peopled scene (the empty triclinium diagram is the
  ceiling), or any appearance of the risen Christ.
- Jesus, God, the Holy Spirit, angels, demons, or any heavenly being.
- Symbolic visions: Revelation 4–22 imagery, Ezekiel 1, Daniel 7–8,
  Zechariah's visions. (Diagram/map lanes in visionary books only, per the
  art direction.)
- Doctrine, discourse, and genealogy (John 3:16, Romans 8, Matthew 1) — there
  is nothing physical to explain.

When a wanted concept sits next to an excluded moment, anchor on the setting
verse, title it after the place or object, and confirm the one-verse-earlier
test still passes.

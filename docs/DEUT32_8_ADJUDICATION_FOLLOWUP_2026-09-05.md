## New primary image consultation

The IAA identifies [B-359054](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-359054)
and [B-359055](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-359055)
as **4Q37, plate 172, fragment 10, recto**, photographed by Shai Halevi in
April 2013. The first is full-spectrum color, the second infrared. Both actual
images were viewed in the normal archive viewer. Their parchment outline,
mounting strip, damage and three surviving line remnants correspond. The IR
image was enlarged and panned to inspect the bottom writing, including the
left-hand end of the divine designation. Visible writing is compatible with
the published `בני אלוהים`; no new letters or alternative reading are claimed.
The color view was an overview, not an equally detailed letter-by-letter check.

The route was a specific image-ID lead in the search result for a
[2016 B-Hebrew discussion](https://bhebrew.biblicalhumanities.org/viewtopic.php?t=811),
followed by direct institutional object verification. The forum itself timed
out on opening; its attribution is discovery evidence only. Its link does
not establish the DJD numbering. This examination was **not blind**: the
published transcription and suggested identification were known beforehand.

This materially narrows the next acquisition target from an undifferentiated
4Q37 image inventory to a verified color/IR object pair. It does **not** yet
certify that the repository's label “DJD fragment 12” maps to IAA fragment 10.
The versioned transcription labels its section simply `12`, whereas later
sections explicitly use `frg.`. Resolve the section/column/fragment convention
in DJD rather than silently upgrading the label. The IAA manuscript page lists

No archive pixels were downloaded, enhanced, generated or added to Git. The

## Full-verse consequence: the opening word also needs a decision

Fresh consultation of [Qumran-Digital, version 2026-05-21](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q37/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
confirms section 12 line 13 starts `בהנחי[ל ...]`: the yod is outside brackets,
but final lamed and the following clause through `למספר` are supplied. Line 14
prints the decisive two words outside brackets. This is a published
transcription, not a complete surviving verse or independent second witness.

The existing candidate is:

`בהנחל עליון גוים בהפרידו בני אדם יצב גבלת עמים למספר בני אלוהים׃`

Its English remains: “When the Most High gave the nations their inheritance,
when he separated the sons of man[b], he fixed the boundaries of the peoples
according to the number of the sons of God[a].”

The candidate deliberately retains unpointed WLC outside the final unit. Thus
it retains defective `בהנחל`, rather than reproducing the published plene
`בהנחיל`. This is consistent with its declared composite construction; it
still requires an explicit source-editor decision. The orthographic contrast
alone does not require changing that English clause. A review alternative
would start `בהנחיל` and leave the rest of the candidate and English unchanged.
Neither alternative is selected here. Other retained words are inherited
baseline decisions, and the bracketed middle of 4Q37 cannot certify them.

## Contrary evidence and adjudication boundary

The strongest direct contrary readings remain MT and the pinned Samaritan
control, `בני ישראל`. Contextual fit of a divine referent does not demonstrate
a particular scribe's motive or establish the earliest exact spelling.
The prior primary Greek results must remain component-specific: Fouad 848
supports the sons noun but does not preserve the God/Israel complement;
Amherst 916 supports the angel noun prefix without the following God word;
Cambridge's qualified 106 correcting-hand report points to sons of Israel.
These are inherited findings from the existing receipts, not new collations.
The OHB `בני אל` proposal remains an explicitly unattested exact-Hebrew
conjecture with explanatory potential and a non-unique Greek retroversion.

The new images make the Hebrew working candidate more concretely inspectable,
but do not resolve its priority over MT/SP, the El conjecture, adaptation in an
excerpt collection, or the modern Greek apparatus conflict. Retain the
existing moderate working preference; do not raise confidence or promote it
on this consultation alone.

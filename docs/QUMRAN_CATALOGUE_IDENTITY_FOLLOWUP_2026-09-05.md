## 4Q8: identical labels can point to different content

The current [4Q8](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q8/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21),
[4Q8a](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q8a/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
and [4Q8b](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q8b/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
pages show this content/locator crosswalk:

| Pinned QDR record, zero-based | Qumran-Digital page/locator | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 46, `4Q8a`, f1:1–4 | `4Q8`, frg.1:1–4 | Genesis 1 context, distinctive wording and line/partial-word sequence |
| 47, `4Q8b`, f1:1–2 | `4Q8`, frg.2:1–2 | Genesis 2:17–18 two-line sequence |
| 48, `4Q8c`, f1:1–2 | `4Q8a`, frg.1:1–2 | Abraham/son and Haran snippets; Genesis 12:4–5 parallels |
| 49, `4Q8d`, f1:1 | `4Q8b`, frg.1:1 | Short title-like string and uncertainty at its ending |

Thus the earlier **exact** label matches `4Q8a` and `4Q8b` are not safe joins
between projects. The missing `4Q8c`/`4Q8d` labels are not simply missing content.
These four correspondences are stronger discovery leads than label similarity,
but they do not prove physical sameness: shared text and editorial layout can
also depend on common editions. Do not merge QDR records 46 and 47 because one
website groups their content under `4Q8`.

The historical [inventory fascicle 7](https://lyingpen.uia.no/dssinventoryproject/fascicle7/)
uses yet another grouping: `4Q8` h1, `4Q8a` h2, `4Q8b` paraphrase, `4Q8c` title.
Its introduction explicitly calls the inventory preliminary. It supplies
historical photo leads (42.723, 43.352, 41.996, 43.157 respectively), not a
current cross-project identity verdict. The next check is DJD XII plus exact
current IAA plate/fragment correspondence. A title indexed at Genesis 1:1 must
not become preserved continuous verse evidence.

## 4Q54b and 4Q69c: one published locator, two possible book assignments

The [4Q54b](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q54b/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
and [4Q69c](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q69c/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
pages cross-reference each other in all four main lines, together with the same
`Add PAM-42.082-2-4` locator. Their parallels include 2 Kings 19:27–29 and
Isaiah 37:28–30. This is **published locator equivalence**, not two independent
witnesses. Keep both catalogue labels but hold them as one evidence unit for
counting until adjudicated; Kings versus Isaiah remains unresolved here.

[Puech's 2012 publisher record](https://poj.peeters-leuven.be/content.php?id=3206356&journal_code=RQ&url=article)
confirms the competing Kings/Isaiah identification in the subtitle, pp.467–472,
DOI `10.2143/RQ.25.3.3206356`. Its full argument was not read. Fresh image/edition
inspection, not shared text alone, is required before adopting physical identity.

## 4Q483: record duplication is not duplicate evidence

QDR has two distinct records with label `4Q483`: ordinal2 has f1:1–3 and f2:1–2;
ordinal209 has f1:4–5. They have different record hashes. They are not byte
duplicates, but neither disjoint numbering nor a common label proves a safe
merge or two objects.

The [current published transcription](https://lexicon.qumran-digital.org/transcriptions/4Q483/2026-05-21/index.html?v=2026-05-21)
has f1:1–3 and f2:1–2. Its first two f1 snippets correspond to QDR ordinal2's
f1:2–3 rather than the same line numbers. QDR's extensive supplied contexts
give much broader verse-reference tags than preserved words. Ordinal209's
Genesis 1:29 context is also largely supplied and not newly verified here.
Preserve both ordinals, prohibit independent voting, and verify conversion
history/edition provenance before merging or discarding either.

The [IAA page](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/4Q483-1)
retains a Genesis-or-Jubilees question in its title. Detailed catalogue fields
did not render in this text fetch; no material/date/plate claim is inferred
from those missing fields.

## 4Q103a: missing from this index is not unattested

A search-only Proverbs PDF lead suggested assignment differences, but was not
read and its claims were not adopted.

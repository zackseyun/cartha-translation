Checked 2026-09-05. This is a dated identity-level screen, not a complete
Isaiah collation or a current census of every discovered source.

QDR biblical index: the two cave-1 copies, eighteen cave-4 copies, 5Q3 and
Mur 3. Six separately catalogued Isaiah pesharim have no queried labels in
that file. They are a concrete additional acquisition queue.

## Sources and identity checks

Tov's author-hosted chapter, *The Text of Isaiah at Qumran*, revised in 2008,
section 1, PDF pp. 1–2, lists 21 Qumran copies and then Mur 3. The complete
two pages and notes were rendered and visually inspected. Note 1 cautions
that fragment/hand-based counts can change: different hands do not necessarily
mean different scrolls. The table is a discovery baseline, not a 2026
authentication statement or all-source denominator.
[Author-hosted chapter](http://www.emanueltov.info/docs/papers/05.isaiah.2008.pdf?v=1.0).

The 2011 SBL volume *Celebrating the Dead Sea Scrolls*, printed xviii, xx and
xxi / PDF 18, 20 and 21, supplies numerical/siglum crosswalks and five cave-4
pesharim. All three relevant pages were visually inspected. It identifies
1Q8 with the Hebrew University Isaiah scroll; these are not two copies.
The inserted 4Q62a corresponds to Isaiah i, so subsequent letters must not
be generated by a simple numerical offset. Its selected abbreviations page
omits 4Q68; the IAA record supplies that explicit crosswalk to Isaiah o.
Unrelated incorrect book labels near the top of SBL p. xx are not adopted.
[SBL front matter](https://www.sbl-site.org/assets/pdfs/pubs/063530P.front.pdf),
[IAA 4Q68](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/4Q68-1).

The six commentary targets are 3Q4 and 4Q161–165. SBL and individual IAA
records establish this acquisition list, not their exact surviving biblical
wording. Pesharim alternate quoted text with interpretation: a useful quotation
must be separated from commentary, adaptation, paraphrase and supplied letters.
The USC project describes this distinction for 4Q162 and its Isaiah 5 material.
[IAA 3Q4](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/3Q4-1),
[USC West Semitic Research Project](https://dornsife.usc.edu/wsrp/isaiah-pesher/).

The `pap` in 4Q69 and 4Q163 denotes papyrus, not Greek. Their roles must not
be confused with the Greek biblical papyri encountered in the Torah work.
Likewise, absence from this biblical-only JSON file is not absence from the
whole QDR project, other databases, physical collections or every POB folder.

# 4Q120 at Leviticus 2:7-8: discriminating evidence check

Date: 2026-09-05. Follow-up to the
[Leviticus catalogue audit](LEVITICUS_CATALOGUE_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-05.md).
Outcome: the consulted published partial word does not independently decide
the person of the bringing verb. No Hebrew or English change is justified by
this evidence alone.

## Evidence actually inspected

Nicholas Eugene Wagner, *The Grammarian's Bible: Scholarship in the Margins
of the Septuagint* (Duke dissertation, 2021), III.3.2, printed p. 119,
identifies 802 / TM62291 and marks the passage `2:7-8(?)`. Its terminal-word
boundary is `π̣ρ̣οϲ[οίϲει`: pi and rho are underdotted, and the completion after
sigma is supplied. This is the author's published profile, not our own reading
of manuscript pixels or a fresh consultation of DJD IX.

The [institutional PDF](https://dukespace.lib.duke.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/12216d72-2eae-47a5-baae-faafc85c8d21/content)
was downloaded privately, SHA256
`d25edc2e0fc178e79eb0f734686746000d80c2e09bce031cae3f798a00a2d9ce`.
PDF pages 134-137 (printed 119-122) were text-extracted, rendered and visually
inspected in full. The PDF skill's visual review confirmed the underdots,
bracket boundary and uncertain reference instead of accepting flattened OCR.
The 922-page dissertation as a whole was not reviewed. Copyright remains with
the author; the complete PDF and renders are not in Git.

The [Göttingen Leviticus introduction](https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/gopher/text/religion/biblical/lxxvar/active/Leviticus,%20ed.%20Sigrid%20Peterson.html),
originally Wevers 1986, author's English translation edited online by Sigrid
Peterson in 2007, lists 802 at A.I.4 with very fragmentary coverage including
2:7-8. We consulted that introduction, not its running critical apparatus.
Its broader witness list also distinguishes uncollated/unavailable manuscripts
and warns against arguments from silence for faded manuscript 707. A critical
edition's coverage therefore needs its own audit; the edition name alone does
not certify all known sources or all legible readings.

## Inference and strongest counter-explanation

Removing diacritics and normalizing lunate sigma solely for comparison gives
the reported unbracketed prefix `προσ`. Both illustrative forms `προσοίσει`
(third singular) and `προσοίσεις` (second singular) fit it. These are a
morphological contrast to test the surviving information, **not two newly
attested apparatus variants**. Supplying the former's ending does not make
that ending surviving ink.

The strongest counter-consideration is that line space, another surviving
portion, or the full apparatus might still constrain the reading. This short
contents profile is not a complete diplomatic line. Consequently the result
is limited: the quoted prefix supplies no independent person distinction;
we have not proved that every available feature of 4Q120 is irrelevant.

There is a second alignment issue. The previously consulted Greek control has
two bringing verbs in 2:8. The profile alone does not establish which occurrence
is represented by this terminal boundary. Nor would identifying a Greek
third-person form automatically recover the Hebrew consonants behind it.
The [earlier agency review](LEVITICUS_2_8_AGENCY_REVIEW_2026-09-05.md) remains
the separate analysis of source pointing and contextual English agents.

## Image route and access limits

The live IAA [B-503625 page](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/image/B-503625)
was opened in a separate research browser tab. It identifies plate 376,
fragment 3, 4Q120, infrared recto, July 2015, Shai Halevi. This verifies a
catalogue route, not a passage assignment: the target pixels were not inspected
and fragment 3 was not established as the relevant 2:7-8 fragment. No image
crop or new transcription is claimed.

The web tool could not open this image URL, but the public browser page was
accessible. A bare numeric-ID search returned unrelated results; those were
discarded. Searches for the primary edition did not establish lawful full-text
access to DJD IX. A book review, an aggregator or a copied Wikipedia narrative
was not substituted for the edition.

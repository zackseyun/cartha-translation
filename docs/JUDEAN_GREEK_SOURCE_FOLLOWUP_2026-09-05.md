## A substantive correction to the existing registry

The current [Göttingen Ra 943 record](https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/catalogue/Ra_943/)
reports competing one- and two-scroll interpretations. Our former instruction
to keep everything under one physical object was too definite. The ID now
remains a catalogue umbrella with the physical-count question open; neither
merging nor splitting supplies automatic independent votes. The receipt
identifies the competing positions and the actual consultation scope.

## What should and should not enter comparison

The new registry targets are Greek evidence. First establish a fragment's
preserved Greek and hand, then compare that reading with book-specific critical
editions and relevant witnesses. Only after accounting for translation and
revision can a possible Hebrew Vorlage be assessed. A Greek witness to one
chapter supplies no attestation at another chapter simply because both are
in the same book. Registration does not promote a Greek form to earliest
attainable Hebrew or imply that its date proves textual priority.

7Q2 remains a separately identified Letter of Jeremiah lead, not a witness to
the canonical Jeremiah book. POB has a distinct deuterocanonical directory;
this classification does not alter the project's canon. The catalogue's
uncertain 4Q127 composition and the unidentified/disputed leads in the receipt
remain outside automatic continuous-biblical-text collation. The IAA
[4Q127 entry](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/4Q127-1)
itself retains question marks in its classifications. A plausible proposed
identification or generative completion cannot supply missing ancient ink.

## Access failures, verification and next action

The PDF skill was read for the attempted Tov survey consultation, but the
author-hosted PDF failed web retrieval and TLS certificate verification; its
non-www hostname did not resolve. TLS verification was not disabled. No
PDF was inspected and no source-file hash is claimed. Publisher bibliographic
metadata located the collection, not its full chapter. Guessed padded
Göttingen routes and Trismegistos target routes also failed. These failed
routes do not establish absent manuscripts. The accessible rendered Ra 943
record, IAA records and Kraft HTML supplied the evidence actually used.

The registry is now 31 mixed objects/editions/families, still with 20 physical
passage-coverage records, 13 formal cases and one unpromoted selection. Four
new registrations do not add four completed collations. The new integrity
tests check relationships, consultation boundaries, counts and actual record
links; they cannot establish the scholarly truth of an ancient reading.

Next: inspect the exact DJD units and any later reassessments; resolve image
regions and rights; record the preserved/uncertain/supplied Greek separately;
then perform passage-level collation. Resolve the Ra 943 competing physical
models through the cited scholarship before changing object counts. Expand
beyond these selected units to other catalogued Greek collections and all-book

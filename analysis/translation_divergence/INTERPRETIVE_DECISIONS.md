# SPOB interpretive decision and influence audit

This file separates a disputed reading from a project-level blocker. SPOB may
select a preferred source/context reading in the main text when the evidence is
strong enough, provided that a live alternative remains visible in a footnote
and the structured record explains the expansion.

## William Branham influence audit

The current SPOB doctrine, drafting prompt, and reviewer prompt do **not** direct
the model to translate according to William Branham or any other named teacher.
A repository scan found no Branham-specific system embedded in SPOB main text,
including serpent-seed language, a seventh-church-age messenger framework, or a
Malachi 4 end-time prophet claim.

The phrase “stay spiritually awake” in 1 Peter 5:8 was selected explicitly by
the project editor and is independently supported in its record by the immediate
devil/roaring-lion context and POB's lexical rationale. It may be compatible
with language used by many preachers, including Branham, but it is not marked as
a Branham interpretation because the record does not depend on him.

Luke 1:17 and Matthew 11:14 retain canonical Elijah/Malachi connections that are
stated by the biblical texts themselves. Their presence is not evidence of a
Branham-specific interpretive layer.

If a named interpreter ever materially affects an SPOB proposal, the record must
identify that person under `interpretive_expansions.external_witnesses`, show the
independent textual evidence, and preserve disputed alternatives. A reading that
depends on a teacher's wider doctrinal system cannot control SPOB main text.
